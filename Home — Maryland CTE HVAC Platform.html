How It Works

  1. Teacher assigns a standard and the platform generates a targeted reflection prompt.
  2. Student writes their evidence response in the Student Portal.
  3. Claude AI evaluates the response against the NCCER rubric and identifies gaps.
  4. Teacher reviews the evaluation and approves the summative score.
  5. The record is sealed on the hash-chain ledger — tamper-evident and transparent.
