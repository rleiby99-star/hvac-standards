a prototype for a competency-based education platform that integrates with a blockchain ledger. The system should allow teachers to input standard rubrics and provide targeted prompts for student reflections. It should use AI to analyze student evidence, evaluate it against the standards, and then securely record the summative data on the blockchain to ensure transparency and prevent tampering."

How It Works
    Teacher assigns a standard and the platform generates a targeted reflection prompt.
    Student writes their evidence response in the Student Portal.
    Claude AI evaluates the response against the NCCER rubric and identifies gaps.
    Teacher reviews the evaluation and approves the summative score.
    The record is sealed on the hash-chain ledger — tamper-evident and transparent.
    R. P. Leiby - Prototype 1 HVAC Standards
